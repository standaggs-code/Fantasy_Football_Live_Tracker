Feature Guide & FAQ

How do I use this?

Just type your exact Sleeper username into the box at the top left and click Load. The app automatically finds all your active leagues for the current season. Use the dropdown menu below your username to instantly switch between your different leagues. No passwords, logins, or cookies required.

What is the difference between the two feeds?

  • Fantasy Alerts: Filters the live NFL play-by-play data to only show plays involving players on your active roster, your bench, or your opponent's roster.
  • All Plays: Shows every single play happening in any real-world NFL game that contains at least one player from either of your fantasy matchups.

What do the numbers next to the player names mean?

Every player badge includes a running score formatted as (Actual / Projected) [Game Clock]. The actual score updates automatically based on live Sleeper data, and the projected score is pulled directly from Sleeper's weekly projections for your specific league's scoring settings. Your team's total active score is aggregated at the top of the roster list.

What does the flashing dot next to the "Live Tracker" logo mean?

This is your global status beacon:

  • Static Grey: Idle. None of your tracked games are currently active.
  • Pulsing Green: Active. At least one game featuring your players is currently being played.
  • Pulsing Red: Red Zone! A tracked game currently has a team inside the 20-yard line.
